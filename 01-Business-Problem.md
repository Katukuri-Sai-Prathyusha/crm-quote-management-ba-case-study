# Business Problem

## Background

The CRM system is used by sales and commercial teams to create and manage customer quotations.

Customers may require additional products or services to be presented as optional items within a quotation. However, the current quotation process does not provide a clear way to distinguish optional products from standard products.

## Problem Statement

Optional products are currently included in the quotation's Net Total even when customers do not intend to purchase them.

This can result in incorrect quotation totals and requires users to perform manual calculations or provide additional clarification to customers.

## Business Impact

The current limitation may result in:

- Incorrect quotation totals
- Manual calculation and adjustment of quotation values
- Increased risk of pricing errors
- Additional clarification between sales teams and customers
- Reduced flexibility when preparing quotations
- Additional effort for users managing complex quotations

## Business Objective

The objective of this enhancement is to allow users to identify products as either Standard or Optional within a quotation.

Optional products should remain visible in the quotation but should not contribute to the Net Total.

## Expected Business Outcome

The enhancement should:

- Allow users to mark products as Optional
- Display optional products clearly within the quotation
- Exclude optional products from the Net Total
- Allow users to change products between Standard and Optional
- Reduce manual calculations
- Improve quotation accuracy and flexibility
