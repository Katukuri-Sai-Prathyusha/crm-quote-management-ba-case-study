# CRM Quote Management Enhancement – Business Analyst Case Study

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the Business Analysis lifecycle for a CRM quotation management enhancement.

The enhancement focuses on allowing users to mark products as optional within a quotation while ensuring that optional products do not contribute to the Net Total.

## Business Problem

Customers need the ability to include optional products in quotations without having those products included in the final quotation total.

## Business Objectives

To define business and functional requirements for an enhancement that allows users to:

- Add products to quotations
- Mark products as Standard or Optional
- Display optional products in quotations
- Exclude optional products from the Net Total
- Convert products between Standard and Optional
