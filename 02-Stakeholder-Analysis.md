# Stakeholder Analysis

## Project

CRM Quote Management Enhancement

## Purpose

Identify the key stakeholders involved in gathering, defining, developing, testing, and implementing the quotation enhancement.

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Responsibilities | Level of Involvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer / End User | Business User | Provides business needs, expectations, and feedback | High |
| Sales Team | End User | Creates quotations and uses the enhanced functionality | High |
| Business Analyst | Requirements Owner | Gathers requirements, analyzes needs, creates PRD and user stories | High |
| Product Manager | Product Owner | Reviews requirements, prioritizes enhancements, and provides product direction | High |
| Development Team | Technical Team | Reviews requirements and develops the enhancement | High |
| QA Team | Testing Team | Validates the functionality against requirements and acceptance criteria | High |
| Business Management | Business Stakeholder | Reviews business impact and provides approval when required | Medium |

## Business Analyst Responsibilities

The Business Analyst acts as the link between business stakeholders and the technical team.

Key responsibilities include:

- Conduct requirement gathering sessions
- Understand customer and business needs
- Ask clarification questions
- Analyze existing functionality
- Identify gaps and improvement opportunities
- Document business and functional requirements
- Create PRDs
- Create user stories
- Define acceptance criteria
- Communicate requirements to development and QA teams
- Support UAT activities
- Validate that the implemented solution meets business requirements
